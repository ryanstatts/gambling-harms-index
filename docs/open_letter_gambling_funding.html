Gambling Industry Donations to Political Parties and MPs: A Public Health Loophole That Must Be Closed

This open letter calls on political parties to voluntarily decline donations, gifts and hospitality from commercial gambling interests.

Delivered to party leaders, ministers and parliamentarians on 1 October 2026, and supported by researchers, charities, recovery organisations, people with lived experience, and young people.
